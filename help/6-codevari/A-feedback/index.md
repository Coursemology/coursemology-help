title: 6a. Automated Feedback using Codaveri
---

You may use Codaveri for automated grading feedback for coding questions.

First, you will need to enable **Codaveri Evaluation and Feedback** under **Course Settings**.

Note that you may enable for specific assessment, or specific question.

![6A-1]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/6A-1.png)

Once a student has submitted the assessment (i.e. finalized submission), Codaveri will generate the feedback automatically for the programming questions. Instructors will have to rate the feedback before it will be published to students.

![6A-2]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/6A-2.png)

Instructors also have the option to edit the feedback if they are not satisfied with Codaveri's feedback. Editing will only be available after you have rated the Codaveri feedback.

The feedback will be seen by student after instructor has published the grade.

![6A-3]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/6A-3.gif)
